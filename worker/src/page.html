[1]Semantic citations

Read every citation of a paper

Paste an arXiv link. Each paper that cites it is opened, the sentences that mention it are pulled out, and a model labels why it was cited and what the authors made of it.

New here?

Each bar is one citation, grouped by the paper it appears in. A hollow bar means the model was less than 90% confident.

Citing papers and their full text come from OpenAlex. Labels come from OpenAI's Decisions API, which returns a probability for each predefined answer. The site reads up to 30 citing papers per paper, most-cited first, and can only read those with open full text.

The labels are a model's reading, and it leans cautious. In a hand-checked test of 20 citations it matched a human on 14 for the reason and 16 for the attitude, and every miss was a retreat to “background” or “neutral”. Answers above 90% confidence matched every time.
