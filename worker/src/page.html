[1]Semantic citations

Read every citation of a paper

Paste an arXiv link. Each paper that cites it is opened, the sentences that mention it are pulled out, and a model labels why it was cited and what the authors made of it.

New here?

Each bar is one citation, grouped by the paper it appears in. A hollow bar means the model was less than 90% confident.

The list of citing papers comes from OpenAlex. Their text comes from the HTML versions arXiv publishes, so only citing papers that are on arXiv can be read, and what is read is the arXiv version. Papers are read most-cited first, and each is read once and then kept for every visitor. Labels come from OpenAI's Decisions API, which returns a probability for each predefined answer.

The labels are a model's reading, and it leans cautious. In a hand-checked test of 14 citations it matched a human on 11 for the reason and 13 for the attitude. Most misses were a retreat to “background” or “neutral”.
